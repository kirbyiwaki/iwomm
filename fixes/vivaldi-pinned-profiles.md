title: Stop Chromium Browsers From Eating RAM in Background
tags: browser, chrome, edge, performance
---
By default, Chromium-based browsers keep background extensions and processes alive after you close the window.

1. Open `chrome://settings/system` (or `edge://settings/system`).
2. Toggle **OFF** *"Continue running background apps when Google Chrome is closed"*.
3. Enable **Memory Saver** under the **Performance** tab.
